**Azure Linux 4.0** is generally available for Azure Virtual Machines and VM Scale Sets. It is Microsoft's first-party open-source Linux distribution, built for Azure, and was introduced at Ignite.

Teams that choose base images for VM fleets or scale sets, and those who want a vendor-supported distribution with Azure integration, should review it.

Deploy a test scale set from the Azure Linux 4.0 image, check that your agents and tooling work, and decide whether it becomes a candidate for your standard image list.
