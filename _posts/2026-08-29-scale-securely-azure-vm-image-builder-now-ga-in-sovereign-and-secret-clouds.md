Maintaining parity across highly regulated and air-gapped environments is no longer a manual hurdle for infrastructure teams. In an era where **security and consistency** are paramount, the gap between public and sovereign cloud capabilities is closing fast, allowing for unified DevOps workflows across the most sensitive regions.

Azure VM Image Builder has officially reached **General Availability (GA)** in Azure Government, China North 3, and—most critically—Azure Government **Secret and Top Secret** regions. This update empowers organizations to automate their "golden image" creation process using a managed service that handles the heavy lifting of patching and configuration. By leveraging this tool, you can ensure that every virtual machine is built to strict compliance standards, reducing the attack surface across your sovereign and air-gapped workloads.
