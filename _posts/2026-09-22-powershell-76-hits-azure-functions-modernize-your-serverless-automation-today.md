Automation just got a major upgrade. **Azure Functions support for PowerShell 7.6 is now generally available**, allowing you to leverage the latest language enhancements and performance improvements in your serverless applications. For developers and IT professionals, this means no more compromises between your local development environment and your production cloud workloads.

By updating to PowerShell 7.6, you gain access to a more robust scripting experience, improved error handling, and optimized resource management. Whether you are building complex CI/CD pipelines or simple event-driven tasks, the ability to **develop locally and deploy seamlessly** to Azure Functions plans ensures a faster time-to-market and a more secure operational posture. It’s time to move beyond legacy scripts and embrace the future of cloud-native automation.
