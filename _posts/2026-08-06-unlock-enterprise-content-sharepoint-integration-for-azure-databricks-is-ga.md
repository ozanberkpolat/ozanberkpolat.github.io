Is your organization’s most valuable intelligence trapped in document silos? For too long, enterprise content stored in SharePoint existed in a vacuum, disconnected from the high-performance analytics and AI capabilities of the modern data lake. **That era of fragmentation is officially over.**

The **SharePoint connector for Azure Databricks** has reached **General Availability**, powered by the robust *Lakeflow Connect* framework. This milestone allows engineering teams to seamlessly ingest files directly into the Databricks Data Intelligence Platform, unifying unstructured document data with your existing AI workflows. Whether you are building sophisticated **RAG-based applications** or automating large-scale document processing, this integration provides a scalable, native path to transform "hidden" content into actionable enterprise insights.

Ready to bridge the gap between your document libraries and your data lake?
