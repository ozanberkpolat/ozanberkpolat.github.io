Legacy migrations often hit a wall when faced with **hard-coded IP dependencies**. For many organizations, re-addressing an application or refactoring its configuration adds unacceptable risk, cost, and regression testing to an already tight migration window. If your data center exit is stalled by a single, unglamorous constraint, it’s time for a more strategic architectural bridge.

This update explores a powerful pattern using **Azure Private Link Service Direct Connect**. By leveraging Private Endpoints, migrated applications can reach on-premises dependencies using the exact same private addresses they’ve always used—even in complex scenarios with **overlapping address spaces**. This solution allows you to move application tiers immediately, preserving business continuity and avoiding costly remediation during the critical first phase of your cloud journey.
