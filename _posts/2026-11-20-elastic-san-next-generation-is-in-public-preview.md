**Elastic SAN Next Generation** is in public preview. It keeps the single SAN resource hierarchy that consolidates block storage workloads and shares performance dynamically across volumes, and it builds on the current offering.

Storage and infrastructure engineers who run Elastic SAN, or who consider it for VMware, AKS or database workloads, should read the preview details for limits and pricing changes.

Deploy a test SAN, benchmark your real workloads and compare cost with managed disks. Do not move production volumes until GA.
