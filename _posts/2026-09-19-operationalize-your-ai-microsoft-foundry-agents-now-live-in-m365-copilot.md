An AI agent’s true power is measured by its **accessibility**. For developers using Microsoft Foundry, the gap between building a sophisticated tool and getting it into the hands of users just disappeared. The hurdle of finding a native operational path has finally been cleared.

The **General Availability** of publishing Microsoft Foundry agents to **Microsoft 365 Copilot** and **Teams** marks a pivotal shift for enterprise developers. This update allows you to deploy custom agents directly into the communication hubs where work actually happens. By removing the friction of manual deployment paths, you can now transform isolated logic into collaborative powerhouses that drive real-world value across the entire Microsoft ecosystem.
