**Zone resilience for Azure VMs** lets you recover an eligible zonal VM in another availability zone in the same region. The existing VM resource and its configuration are kept. ZRS managed disks replicate data synchronously across zones to support this.

Infrastructure and operations teams with single-zone VMs should care. It offers zone-level recovery without rebuilding the VM or running a second copy.

Check which VMs are eligible and whether they use ZRS disks. Try a recovery test in a lab subscription and compare it with your current zonal failover runbooks.
