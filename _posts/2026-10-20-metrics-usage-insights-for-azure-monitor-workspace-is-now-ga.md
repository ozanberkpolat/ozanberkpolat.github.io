Do you actually know which metrics are driving usage in your Azure Monitor workspace? Metrics usage insights is now **generally available**, giving you much better visibility into what consumes your metrics budget.

The feature lets you monitor workspace utilization, identify **unused metrics**, understand usage trends, and investigate changes in usage over time. That makes it easier to trim noise, keep costs predictable, and spot the moment a team starts sending far more data than before.

If you run Prometheus-style metrics at scale, this is worth a look. [Dive into the full technical breakdown here](https://azure.microsoft.com/updates?id=577165)
