Stop drowning in a sea of routing prefixes. As your Azure environment scales, your on-premises routers shouldn't have to bear the burden of tracking every single individual virtual network. The "routing bloat" created by hundreds of specific paths can lead to hardware limitations, management complexity, and increased latency in route propagation.

With the **General Availability** of summarized advertised gateway prefixes, Azure empowers you to streamline your hybrid architecture. You can now specify **aggregated (summarized) prefixes** for your Azure gateway to advertise to on-premises networks. Instead of flooding your local infrastructure with micro-routes, you can present a clean, consolidated CIDR block. 

> This update is a essential for enterprise-scale networking, ensuring your on-premises routing tables remain lean, performant, and easy to audit.
