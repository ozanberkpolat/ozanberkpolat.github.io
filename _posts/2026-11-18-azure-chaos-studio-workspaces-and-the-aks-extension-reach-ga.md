**Azure Chaos Studio Workspaces** and the Azure Chaos Studio extension for Azure Kubernetes Service are now generally available. Chaos Studio is the managed service for introducing controlled failures into applications to test how they respond and recover. The AKS extension brings those experiments to Kubernetes workloads.

Reliability and platform engineers should care, particularly those preparing for zone or regional failure drills. It fits alongside Azure Infrastructure Resiliency Manager, also announced as GA.

Pick one AKS workload with a defined recovery target. Run a small fault experiment in a non-production cluster, record what actually happens, and compare it with your documented recovery objective.
