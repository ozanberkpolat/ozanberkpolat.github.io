The era of high-scale computing just got a massive upgrade. Azure has officially announced the **General Availability** of its latest Dl, D, and E v7 virtual machine sizes, providing the raw power needed for the world’s most demanding enterprise workloads. If you are struggling with compute-heavy bottlenecks, this is the update you have been waiting for.

Powered by the next-generation **Intel® Xeon® 6 processors**, these VMs come in staggering **248 and 372 vCPU** configurations. This isn't just about scale; it's about pure efficiency. These general-purpose and memory-optimized instances deliver up to a **20% performance boost** over previous Intel-based v6 VMs. This ensures that your massive databases, complex analytics, and mission-critical applications run faster and more reliably than ever before.
