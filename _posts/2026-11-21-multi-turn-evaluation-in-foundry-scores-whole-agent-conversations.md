**Multi-turn evaluation** is in public preview in Microsoft Foundry. It assesses complete agent conversations against your quality standards, not only single responses. A reply can look correct alone and still belong to a conversation that fails the user.

Engineers who build and test agents should care. Single-response checks can miss failures that only show up across several turns, such as lost context or drift from the goal.

Try it on your existing agent test conversations and compare the results with your per-response evaluations. Because it is a preview, treat the scores as an extra signal for now, not a release gate.
