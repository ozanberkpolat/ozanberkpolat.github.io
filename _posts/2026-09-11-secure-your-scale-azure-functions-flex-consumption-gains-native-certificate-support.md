**Security meets seamless scalability.** Azure Functions Flex Consumption has reached a major milestone with the General Availability of TLS/SSL certificate support. For developers building high-stakes serverless applications, this update provides the essential infrastructure for robust, encrypted communication without compromising on elastic performance.

This release introduces a streamlined **site-scoped certificate model**, allowing each function app to manage up to three private (`.pfx`) and three public (`.cer`) certificates. Whether you are uploading files directly for rapid deployment or importing them from the hardened environment of **Azure Key Vault**, the process is now natively integrated into your serverless workflow. This update ensures that your apps remain compliant and secure, no matter how high they scale.
