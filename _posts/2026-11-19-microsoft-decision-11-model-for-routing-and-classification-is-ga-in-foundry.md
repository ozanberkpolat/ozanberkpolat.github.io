**Microsoft-Decision-1.1** is a small model built for structured decisions in software: routing, classification, prioritization, verification and workflow control. It picks among actions the developer permits and returns calibrated confidence scores, so an application can defer when uncertainty is high. It is built on Gemma 4 26B A4B and is GA in Microsoft Foundry, OpenRouter and Vercel.

Architects who send every small decision to a large LLM should look at it. It targets cost, latency and control, and leaves larger models for complex reasoning. The price is the same as Microsoft-Decision-1.

Test it on an existing routing or triage step. Compare accuracy, latency and cost with your current model, and use the confidence score to decide when to escalate.
