**Database Hub in Fabric** is generally available. It gives one place to discover, monitor and manage databases across Azure, Fabric, on-premises environments and other clouds, so admins can spot bottlenecks and apply governance more consistently.

Database agents are in preview inside the hub. They observe operational signals, detect engine-specific issues, explain them and recommend fixes or carry out approved actions. They work within existing identity permissions and approval boundaries.

Database and platform teams with mixed estates should try the hub for inventory first. Test the agents on non-production databases and review which actions you would allow them to run.
