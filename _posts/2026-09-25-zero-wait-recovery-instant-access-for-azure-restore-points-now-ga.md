Are your recovery time objectives (RTO) under constant pressure? The traditional bottleneck of waiting for lengthy data transfers before initiating a restore is officially a thing of the past. Azure has announced the **General Availability** of **Instant Access** for application-consistent restore points, providing a massive boost to business continuity for high-performance workloads.

This feature is a game-changer for virtual machines leveraging **Premium SSD v2** or **Ultra Disks**. Instead of waiting for snapshot data to move, you can now begin restoring disks the moment the snapshot is created. This **immediate availability** significantly slashes downtime, allowing your team to focus on recovery rather than watching progress bars. Whether you are managing massive databases or complex enterprise applications, this update ensures your data is ready exactly when you need it most.
