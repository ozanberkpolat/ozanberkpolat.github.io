Azure is widening its **confidential computing** portfolio to protect data while it is processed, not only at rest and in transit. Three offerings are now in preview, one of them VR72 multi-GPU confidential computing. The announcement frames this around AI workloads and data collaboration with more control.

Security and architecture teams that hold regulated data or want to run AI on sensitive datasets should care. The related Azure Updates entries also show Confidential VMs with Intel TDX on AKS as GA and OS disk encryption for AKS confidential node pools in preview.

List the workloads where in-use protection is a stated requirement. Match them to the preview offerings and plan a test, keeping in mind that preview services carry no production SLA.
