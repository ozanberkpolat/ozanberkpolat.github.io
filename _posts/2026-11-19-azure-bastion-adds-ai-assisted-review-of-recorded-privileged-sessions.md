**Azure Bastion RDP and SSH vision** is in public preview. It brings AI-assisted review to recorded privileged sessions, with session summaries and insights into noteworthy and risky administrative activity.

Security and operations teams that must audit admin access to servers should care, since reviewing recordings by hand does not scale.

If you already record Bastion sessions, test it on a sample of them and check how well the flagged activity matches what your reviewers would flag.
