Automating data operations across Blob and Data Lake Storage just got less manual. Azure Storage Actions now ships three enhancements that cut down the busywork of managing tasks at scale.

The update is **generally available** and covers three areas: automatic propagation of task definition updates, new execution controls, and case-sensitive matching. The first means that when you update a storage task definition, the change flows through automatically instead of you chasing every place it is used. The other two give you more say over how and when tasks run and how conditions match your data.

If you run automated data management on Azure Storage, this is worth a few minutes. [Dive into the full technical breakdown here](https://azure.microsoft.com/updates?id=578628)
