Microsoft Foundry adds a learning loop for agents. **Multi-turn evaluation** is generally available and scores whole conversations. A new insights capability, also GA, analyzes production traces, groups recurring failures such as hallucinations and token spikes, and ranks the most frequent issues.

In preview, human annotation lets experts add ratings, comments and expected answers to traces and curate golden datasets. Task-specific rubric evaluators can be generated from those datasets, with safety support in preview. An agent optimizer is also part of the loop.

Teams running agents in production should enable trace collection, then use insights to pick what to fix first. Start building a small golden dataset now so evaluations reflect your own quality bar.
