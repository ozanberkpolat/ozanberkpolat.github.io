Running distributed Python at scale usually means stitching together clusters, schedulers and tooling yourself. **Anyscale on Azure** is now *Generally Available*, and it aims to take that burden off your team.

It is a managed platform for running distributed Python workloads on **Ray**. Instead of living in a separate environment, it deploys directly onto your own **Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS)** cluster and integrates with the Azure services your team already uses. That means Ray workloads can sit next to the rest of your Azure estate rather than beside it.

If you run AI or data-heavy Python on AKS, this is worth a close look. [Dive into the full technical breakdown here](https://azure.microsoft.com/updates?id=573744)
