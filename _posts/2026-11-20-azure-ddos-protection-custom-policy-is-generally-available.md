**Azure DDoS Protection custom policy** is generally available. By default, machine-learning auto-tuning sets detection thresholds per protected resource. Custom policy lets you override those for workloads where the defaults do not fit, such as latency-sensitive services.

Network security teams with unusual traffic profiles should care.

Review mitigation history for resources that saw false positives or late mitigation. Set custom thresholds only for those, and keep auto-tuning for the rest.
