The "Wild West" era of AI development is coming to an end. As enterprises transition from experimental chatbots to production-grade agents, the core challenge has shifted from simple implementation to **enterprise-grade control**. The General Availability of **Unity AI Gateway** on Azure Databricks marks a pivotal moment for organizations needing to bridge the gap between rapid innovation and strict corporate oversight.

This update provides a centralized cockpit for managing AI models, agents, and **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** services under a single governance framework. Platform teams can now enforce granular **access controls**, apply real-time safety guardrails, and monitor usage patterns to prevent cost overruns. By unifying governance, Unity AI Gateway ensures your AI initiatives remain secure and compliant without sacrificing the agility your developers need to build the next generation of intelligent tools.
