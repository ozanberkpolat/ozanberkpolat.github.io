Managing remote access just got a lot tighter. In the high-stakes world of cloud infrastructure, **indefinite access is a liability.** That’s why the general availability of **Azure Bastion shareable link expiration** is a crucial update for administrators who prioritize the principle of least privilege.

> By automating the sunsetting of temporary access, you remove the human error factor from your security posture and ensure that "temporary" actually means temporary.

Administrators can now specify a precise date and time for link expiration during the creation process. Once the configured time passes, the link is immediately rendered useless, preventing any further unauthorized reconnects. Whether you are providing limited-time access to a third-party vendor or a one-time troubleshooting window for a developer, this update provides *automated security* without the manual overhead of revoking permissions.
