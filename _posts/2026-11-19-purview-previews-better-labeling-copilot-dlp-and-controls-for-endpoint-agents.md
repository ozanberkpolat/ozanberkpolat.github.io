New **Microsoft Purview** capabilities in preview cover three areas. Automatic classification and labeling of sensitive data becomes more precise and stays current with less manual tuning. Data loss prevention for Copilot can block grounding on files with sensitive content even when they are unlabeled, and prompt protection extends to attachments, linked files and text. Data security controls also extend to AI agents running on Windows endpoints, governing what data they can access.

Security and compliance engineers rolling out Copilot should care most. The unlabeled-file grounding block closes a common gap in tenants with incomplete labeling.

Review your current Copilot DLP policies and label coverage. Enable the preview in a test scope, and measure false positives on the new auto-labeling before widening it.
