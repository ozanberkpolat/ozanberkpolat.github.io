Scaling end-to-end testing has long been a bottleneck for high-velocity dev teams. As modern web applications grow in complexity, the infrastructure required to validate them often becomes a management nightmare. **Playwright Workspaces** is officially changing that narrative by offering a fully managed, cloud-hosted environment for your automated testing suites.

We are excited to announce that Playwright Workspaces in Azure App Testing is now **Generally Available** in Switzerland North, Japan East, and Australia East. This update brings the power of high-performance cloud browsers to these strategic regions, enabling *massive parallelism* without the overhead of local infrastructure. By leveraging managed browsers, teams can significantly reduce execution time and focus on shipping code rather than debugging test environments.

Ready to accelerate your testing lifecycle and achieve global scale?
