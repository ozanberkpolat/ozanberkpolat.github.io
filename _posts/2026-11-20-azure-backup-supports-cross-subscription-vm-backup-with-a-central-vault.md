**Cross-subscription backup** for Azure VMs is generally available. A VM can be protected by a Recovery Services vault in a different subscription within the same tenant.

Backup and platform teams can now centralize vaults in a dedicated subscription. This simplifies governance, separation of duties and ransomware protection.

Review RBAC for the vault and VM subscriptions, and update Azure Policy that enforces backup. Plan which existing vaults to consolidate.
