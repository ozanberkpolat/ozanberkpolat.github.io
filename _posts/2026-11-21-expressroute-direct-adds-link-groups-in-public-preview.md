**Link groups** for ExpressRoute Direct let you aggregate several links instead of managing multiple independent ExpressRoute connections. The goal is to scale connectivity for high-throughput workloads and reduce operational work.

Network engineers who run ExpressRoute Direct ports and have outgrown a single link, or who run several circuits only for bandwidth, should evaluate it. Fewer connections to manage means less routing and circuit overhead.

Review your current Direct port layout and find circuits that exist only to add capacity. Test link groups in a non-critical environment first, and check limits and failover behavior in the preview documentation.
