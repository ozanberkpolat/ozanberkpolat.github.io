Are you tired of "one-size-fits-all" tooling that doesn't quite align with your team's unique rhythm? The era of compromising your workflow is over. The **Azure Developer CLI (azd) Extension Framework** has officially reached General Availability, transforming the standard CLI from a static tool into a **fully extensible platform** designed to adapt to *you*.

This milestone empowers developers and partners to inject custom capabilities directly into the `azd` ecosystem. By leveraging the framework, you can now build bespoke extensions that automate specialized tasks, integrate proprietary security checks, or streamline complex deployment pipelines. It’s not just about running commands anymore; it’s about creating a modular, high-performance environment that reflects your team’s best practices and specific architectural needs.

Ready to see how you can start building and sharing your own custom extensions?
