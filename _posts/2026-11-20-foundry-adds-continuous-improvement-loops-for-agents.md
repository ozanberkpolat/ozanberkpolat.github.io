Microsoft Foundry now has **continuous improvement** for agents. It helps organizations evaluate, optimize and improve agents over time while keeping teams in control of changes.

This matters to anyone running agents in production, where quality drifts as data, prompts and models change. A built-in loop can replace ad hoc evaluation scripts.

Review which agents already have evaluation sets, decide who approves automated improvements, and try the feature on one low-risk agent first.
