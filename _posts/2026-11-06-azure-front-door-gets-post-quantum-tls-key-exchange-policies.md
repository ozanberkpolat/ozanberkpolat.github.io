Cryptographic threats keep evolving, and the TLS handshake at your edge is where that starts. **Azure Front Door** now lets you control how keys are exchanged, including a hybrid post-quantum option.

The feature is *generally available*. You can configure a TLS key exchange policy on Front Door and choose from predefined options, so you can balance stronger protection against the client compatibility you need to keep. The hybrid post-quantum key exchange is the headline, and it helps you prepare today for what comes next without rebuilding your setup.

If you run public endpoints behind Front Door, this is worth a look before your next security review. [Dive into the full technical breakdown here](https://azure.microsoft.com/updates?id=577668)
