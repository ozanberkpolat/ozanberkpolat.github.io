When a recruiter boils down a complex senior engineer role into a simple "Java AND Cloud" search, the nuances of the hiring manager's vision often vanish. This is the **recruiting context gap**, and it is where talent discovery often fails. How do you ensure that "preferred" skills do not accidentally become "mandatory" filters that exclude top-tier candidates?

This technical update introduces a sophisticated architecture using **Microsoft Foundry** and **Azure AI Search** to bridge that gap. By leveraging **structured outputs** and a curated terminology index, the proposed workflow extracts high-fidelity requirements while keeping the human recruiter in control. You will explore how to implement automated terminology expansion, maintain query fidelity, and use **observability tools** to trace every transformation from natural language to Boolean logic.
