**Microsoft IQ** is a new enterprise context management platform. Its goal is to let agents use an organization's own knowledge and data, not only what the model knows. Microsoft frames the differentiator as everything around the model.

Architects building agents on Foundry or Copilot should care. Grounding and context are where most agent quality and data exposure decisions are made.

Check how your current agents get organizational context today. Then map those sources against what Microsoft IQ covers, and review access controls before connecting any of them.
