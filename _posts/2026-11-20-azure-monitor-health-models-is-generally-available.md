**Azure Monitor Health Models** is generally available. It lets you define workload health as a first-class construct. It aggregates signals from metrics, logs and dependencies into a single health view.

SRE and operations teams who watch many separate alerts per workload should care. A health model shows the state of a whole workload instead of individual resources.

Pick one critical workload and model its dependencies and signals. Compare the resulting health view with your current alerting before you roll it out more widely.
