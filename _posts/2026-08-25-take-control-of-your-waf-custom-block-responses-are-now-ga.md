Stop settling for generic "403 Forbidden" pages that leave your users in the dark. In the high-stakes world of web security, the user experience shouldn't end just because a request was blocked. Azure has officially announced the **General Availability** of custom block response codes and bodies for WAF integrated with Application Gateway. 

This update empowers security engineers and developers to move beyond the defaults. You can now define **custom status codes** and **response bodies** specifically for blocked traffic. Whether you need to redirect users to a support portal, provide detailed instructions for resolving false positives, or simply ensure the error page aligns with your corporate branding, the control is finally in your hands. This isn't just a security update; it's a significant upgrade to your application's professional footprint and user journey management.

Ready to elevate your security posture and your user experience simultaneously?
