**Azure Cobalt 200** VMs are now available. Microsoft positions them for cloud-native applications, databases, developer tools and agentic AI workloads, with better performance and efficiency than the previous generation.

Teams running Arm-compatible workloads, containers or scale-out services should care, mainly for price-performance and capacity planning.

Pick a representative service, benchmark it on Cobalt 200 against your current size, and confirm region and quota availability before planning a migration.
