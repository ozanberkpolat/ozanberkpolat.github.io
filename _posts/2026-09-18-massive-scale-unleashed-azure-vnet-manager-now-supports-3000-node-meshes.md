Managing a handful of virtual networks is simple, but scaling to thousands is an architectural challenge that often leads to management fatigue. The General Availability of **high-scale mesh** connectivity in Azure Virtual Network Manager (AVNM) changes the game for enterprise networking, providing the tools needed to handle massive growth without the traditional complexity of manual peering.

By utilizing the "connected group" feature, architects can now seamlessly link up to **3,000 virtual networks** in a single mesh configuration. This update focuses on removing the bottlenecks of high-scale IP connectivity, allowing for streamlined orchestration across expansive cloud footprints. Whether you are building out global application environments or consolidating disparate corporate segments, this update ensures your network topology can scale as fast as your business requires.

Scale your network further and faster than ever before.
