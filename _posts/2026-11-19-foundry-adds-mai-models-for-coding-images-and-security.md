Microsoft Foundry now lists a first-party **MAI model family**. MAI-Code-2 targets demanding agentic coding and MAI-Code-2-Flash targets high-volume everyday coding. Both are generally available in GitHub, and Flash is in private preview in Foundry. MAI-Image-2.7 and its Flash variant are in preview. MAI-Cyber-2-Flash is in preview and only available inside Codename MDASH. Microsoft-Decision-1.1, for routing, classification and workflow control, is generally available.

Architects who run model catalogs for several teams should care. The split between a full and a Flash model gives a clear cost and latency choice per workload, and the decision model suits cheap gating steps in front of larger models.

Check regional and quota availability in your Foundry projects. Compare MAI-Code-2-Flash against your current coding model on a real workload before changing defaults. Note that the cyber model cannot be used outside MDASH.
