**Azure Cobalt 200** virtual machines, built on Microsoft's own Arm-based processor, are now generally available. They already run in Australia East, Central US, East US, Indonesia Central, Italy North, South Central US, Spain Central, Sweden Central, UK South, West US 2 and West US 3. Microsoft says 24 regions will be covered by the end of the year. The general purpose Dpsv7 and Dplsv7, memory optimized Epsv7, high-memory Mpsv4 and dense storage Lpsv5 series are listed as GA.

Platform and compute teams that size VM fleets should care, especially where cost per core matters. Cobalt 200 is also offered for PostgreSQL Flexible Server. Arm64 means your images, containers and agents need arm64 builds.

Check whether your target regions are in the current list. Then pick one stateless workload with multi-arch images and run a price and performance comparison against your current x64 series.
