**Programmed Training** is an API in Microsoft Foundry for building your own post-training loop over open-weight models. It is in public preview. Access is by request through a preview sign-up form. It sits beside managed training jobs, which suit cases where you only need a standard training run.

Teams that tune open-weight models and need control over the training loop itself should look at it. Managed jobs fit simple cases. This API is for custom logic they can't express.

If you have such a use case, submit the sign-up form and check the access terms. Because it is a preview, keep it out of production plans until it reaches general availability.
