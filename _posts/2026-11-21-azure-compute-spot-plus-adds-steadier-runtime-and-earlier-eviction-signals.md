**Azure Compute Spot plus** is an enhanced tier of Spot Virtual Machines in public preview. It offers more runtime consistency and earlier eviction signals for interruption-tolerant workloads. Standard Spot stays the choice for highly resilient workloads that focus on lowest cost.

Platform and FinOps engineers who run batch, CI or data processing on Spot, and who lose work to sudden evictions, should look at it. A longer warning gives jobs time to checkpoint or drain.

Pick one interruption-tolerant workload that suffers from evictions and run it on the new tier. Compare eviction rate and cost against standard Spot before you decide where each tier fits.
