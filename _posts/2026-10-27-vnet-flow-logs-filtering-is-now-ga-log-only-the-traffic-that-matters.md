Flow logs are only useful when you can find the signal in them. If you run busy Azure virtual networks, you know how fast unfiltered logs pile up in storage and drown the traffic you actually care about.

**Azure Network Watcher VNet flow logs filtering is now generally available.** You decide which flows get logged based on specific criteria: IP addresses, ports, protocols, encryption state, flow state and traffic direction. Instead of capturing everything and sifting through it later, you log just what your investigation or compliance need requires.

Curious how to set up the filters for your own VNets? [Dive into the full technical breakdown here](https://azure.microsoft.com/updates?id=577782)
