If you have ever split a workload across subscriptions just to dodge the storage account limit, this one is for you. The default of 250 accounts per region, per subscription, is no longer the ceiling.

Higher Azure Storage account resource quotas are now **generally available**. A subscription can hold up to **1,000 storage accounts with standard endpoints per region**, up from the default of 250. The point is to manage more accounts inside a single subscription instead of fanning out into extra ones.

Worth a look before your next landing zone or multi-tenant design. [Dive into the full technical breakdown here](https://azure.microsoft.com/updates?id=578358)
