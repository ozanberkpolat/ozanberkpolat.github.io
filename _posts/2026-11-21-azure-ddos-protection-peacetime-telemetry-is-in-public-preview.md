**Peacetime telemetry** for Azure DDoS Protection shows the traffic reaching your protected public IP addresses during normal operation. Before this, you saw it mostly while a mitigation was active.

Security and network operations teams should care. A normal-traffic baseline helps you tune alert thresholds, tell attacks from legitimate spikes and review protection with data.

Enable it on a few protected public IPs and send the data to your monitoring workspace. Build a baseline of typical traffic and compare it with your current alert rules.
