If you run Azure Database for PostgreSQL Flexible Server, your v5 compute choices just got wider. Microsoft announced general availability of **Intel and AMD v5 diskless** compute, new options that skip local temporary storage.

The new SKUs extend the v5 portfolio in two families: **General Purpose** and **Memory Optimized**. The article describes them as SKUs that do not include local temporary disk, so check how your workloads use temp space before you pick one. The release is marked as launched and generally available.
