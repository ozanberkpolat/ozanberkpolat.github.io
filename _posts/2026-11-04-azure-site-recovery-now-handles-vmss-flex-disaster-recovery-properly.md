If you run workloads on Virtual Machine Scale Sets with flexible orchestration, your disaster recovery story just got a lot less manual. Azure Site Recovery now offers an enhanced experience built for **VMSS Flex**, and it is **generally available**.

The update improves **target configuration** and the **end-to-end recovery workflow**, covering failover and the steps that follow it, such as reprotect. In short, the scale set model and Site Recovery now work together instead of you stitching the pieces by hand.

If DR for flexible scale sets was on your to-do list, this is worth a look. [Dive into the full technical breakdown here](https://azure.microsoft.com/updates?id=578926)
