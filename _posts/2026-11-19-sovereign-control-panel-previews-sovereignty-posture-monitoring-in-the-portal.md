**Sovereign Control Panel** is an Azure portal experience in public preview. It helps organizations assess and continuously monitor their sovereignty posture, understand the evidence behind identified gaps, and act on them.

Compliance, governance and architecture teams with data residency or sovereignty requirements should care. It gives one place to see gaps with their supporting evidence.

Open it against a subscription with regulated workloads and check whether its findings match your own sovereignty assessment.
