**Azure Infrastructure Resiliency Manager** is generally available. It offers a goal-driven experience to strengthen application resiliency: start with zonally resilient deployment templates, assess applications at scale, and work on the gaps found.

Architects and operations teams who own availability targets across many applications should look at it. It can turn resiliency reviews from manual checks into a repeatable process.

Run an assessment on a few key applications and compare the results with your current zone and region design documents.
