Securing serverless environments is no longer a "nice-to-have"—it is a critical requirement for modern cloud-native architectures. As container estates expand, managing security across fragmented workflows becomes a major bottleneck for DevOps and Security teams striving for a "single pane of glass" visibility.

With this latest update, you can now bring **Azure Container Apps** environments directly into the **Microsoft Defender for Cloud** Serverless Containers Posture experience. This integration empowers security teams to extend their posture management, identify vulnerabilities, and enforce compliance across their entire container estate from a single, centralized workflow. By bridging the gap between development and security, this update ensures your serverless infrastructure remains resilient without adding operational overhead.

Streamline your security operations and gain full visibility into your serverless infrastructure today.
