Microsoft Foundry now shows **governance policy guidance** inside Foundry Canvas and the Foundry Toolkit for Visual Studio Code. While developers build agents, both tools surface the policies that apply to agent types, models, toolboxes and guardrails, and they warn when a design conflicts with them. The feature is generally available.

Platform and governance teams should care most. Policy problems show up while the agent is being built, not at deployment or in a later review. That cuts rework and makes it harder for a non-compliant agent to reach production.

Check that your Foundry governance policies are defined and assigned at the right scope, so the guidance reflects your real rules. Then tell agent developers to use Canvas or the VS Code toolkit and to treat the warnings as part of their normal workflow.
