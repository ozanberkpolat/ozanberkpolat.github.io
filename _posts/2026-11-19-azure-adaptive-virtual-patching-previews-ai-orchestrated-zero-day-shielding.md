**Azure Adaptive Virtual Patching** is in public preview. It uses an AI-orchestrated Adaptive Shield to reduce exposure to new and newly disclosed zero-day vulnerabilities while you test and deploy the real software patches. The initial workload focus is Linux.

Vulnerability and platform teams who struggle with the gap between disclosure and patch rollout should care. It does not replace patching. It narrows the window of exposure during it.

Try it on a few Linux workloads, and compare it with your current patch SLAs and compensating controls before relying on it.
