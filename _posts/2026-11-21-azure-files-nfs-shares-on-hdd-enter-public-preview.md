Azure Files now supports **NFS shares on HDD** in public preview. It adds a lower-cost, fully managed option next to the existing SSD offering. The target is Linux workloads where cost matters more than latency, such as general-purpose applications, shared datasets and backups.

Storage and Linux platform engineers should look at it for data that does not need SSD performance.

Test a share with a representative workload and compare throughput and cost against SSD. Keep it out of production until the service reaches general availability.
