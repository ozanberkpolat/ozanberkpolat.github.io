The landscape of healthcare technology is shifting, and the bridge between cutting-edge AI and clinical workflows just got significantly shorter. For organizations looking to combat physician burnout and streamline documentation, the friction of procurement has often been the greatest hurdle to innovation—until now.

This latest update introduces the **Microsoft Marketplace** as a primary discovery and procurement channel for Dragon Copilot AI apps and agents. Now, customers in the United States can directly *discover, evaluate, and purchase* specialized Physician Apps through a streamlined enterprise ecosystem. By centralizing these assets, healthcare providers can rapidly deploy sophisticated AI agents that integrate directly into their existing infrastructure, drastically reducing the time between procurement and patient care.
