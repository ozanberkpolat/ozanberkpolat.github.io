Scaling storage used to mean scheduling maintenance windows and bracing for application reboots—but those days are over. Microsoft has officially announced the **General Availability (GA) of Live Resize** for shared Premium SSD v2 (Pv2) and Ultra data disks. This update is a game-changer for high-availability workloads that demand continuous uptime.

With this feature, you can dynamically expand your disk capacity **without any disruption** to your running applications. This allows you to optimize your infrastructure costs by provisioning only what you need today and scaling up instantly as your data footprints grow. No more over-provisioning "just in case"; now, your storage evolves as fast as your business does.

Ready to eliminate the friction from your storage management?
