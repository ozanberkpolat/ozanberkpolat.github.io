As organizations accelerate their transition to IPv6-first architectures, a critical challenge remains: how to maintain communication with an internet that is still predominantly IPv4. This protocol mismatch often creates unnecessary complexity in network design and deployment, forcing engineers to manage cumbersome workarounds.

Azure is addressing this hurdle head-on. The **StandardV2 NAT Gateway** now officially supports **NAT64**, a transformative feature that enables IPv6-only workloads to communicate with IPv4-only destinations effortlessly. By translating synthesized outbound IPv6 traffic into IPv4 traffic—relying on a **DNS64-capable resolver**—this update streamlines your cloud infrastructure, reduces the need for dual-stack configurations, and ensures your modern applications remain fully connected to legacy endpoints.

Stay ahead of the connectivity curve and simplify your network egress strategy today.
