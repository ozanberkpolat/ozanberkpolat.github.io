> In the world of high-velocity data management, **proximity is performance.**

Azure is significantly broadening its regional footprint with the announcement that Lakebase is now **Generally Available** in four major global hubs: *North Central US*, *France Central*, *Germany West Central*, and *East Asia*. This expansion is a game-changer for architects and developers who require localized data presence to minimize latency and optimize the user experience across diverse geographical markets.

This update isn't just about broader coverage; it provides the critical flexibility needed to navigate complex **data residency** requirements and compliance standards across North America, Europe, and Asia. By providing these new deployment options, Lakebase ensures that your cloud-native applications can scale seamlessly while maintaining the high-performance benchmarks your enterprise demands. Whether you are optimizing for European sovereignty or Asian market growth, the tools you need are now closer than ever.
