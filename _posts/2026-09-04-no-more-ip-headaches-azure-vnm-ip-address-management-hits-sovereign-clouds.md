Managing a complex network shouldn't feel like a high-stakes puzzle where one overlapping CIDR block can bring your entire architecture to a standstill. For organizations operating at scale, the manual overhead of tracking IP addresses across thousands of virtual networks is the silent killer of productivity and the primary source of configuration drift.

The wait is finally over for highly regulated and specialized environments. **Azure Virtual Network Manager (AVNM) IP address management (IPAM)** is now **Generally Available** across US Gov (Virginia, Texas, Arizona) and China (North 3, China East 3) regions. This update brings powerful automation to your network topology, allowing for unified, policy-based IP allocation that eliminates the need for error-prone spreadsheets. Whether you are managing sovereign workloads or massive global footprints, you can now ensure *seamless connectivity* and *automated compliance* with just a few clicks.
