In the rapidly evolving landscape of autonomous workflows, maintaining absolute control over your digital agents is no longer optional—it is a **security and operational necessity**. As organizations scale their AI footprint, the risk of unmanaged or "rogue" agents grows, making centralized oversight the cornerstone of a mature cloud strategy.

Microsoft Foundry has addressed this challenge head-on with the General Availability of enhanced governance actions within the **Agent 365 surface**. Administrators can now leverage native enable and disable actions for Foundry agent objects directly through the Microsoft Admin Center. This update provides a unified "kill switch" and deployment lever, allowing teams to gate agent availability across the entire tenant instantly. By centralizing these lifecycle controls, Microsoft is ensuring that your AI workforce remains strictly aligned with organizational compliance and business requirements.
