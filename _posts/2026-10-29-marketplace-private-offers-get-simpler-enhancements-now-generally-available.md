If your team buys software through Microsoft Marketplace, the way you negotiate and consume spend just got a bit easier. Microsoft has announced **general availability** of enhancements to Marketplace private offers.

Private offers let you align pricing and terms with what your organization actually needs, including **flexible billing** and **custom contract lengths**, while making the most of your spending power. The announcement says that for eligible private offers, Microsoft is simplifying the experience further.

Worth a look if you manage procurement or cloud budgets. [Dive into the full technical breakdown here](https://azure.microsoft.com/updates?id=576547)
