The next frontier of high-performance cloud storage has officially arrived. Azure has announced the general availability of the **Lasv5 and Laosv5-series** virtual machines, powered by the formidable **5th Gen AMD EPYC™ "Turin" processors**. For organizations managing data-intensive applications that require extreme throughput and low-latency disk access, this update represents a massive leap forward in infrastructure capability.

These instances are engineered for heavy lifting, with scaling options ranging from **2 to 160 vCPUs**. Each vCPU is backed by **8GiB of memory**, but the real differentiator is the local storage capacity. Each vCPU provides a staggering **720GB of local NVMe disk capacity**, making these VMs the ideal choice for NoSQL databases, big data analytics, and large-scale data warehousing workloads where local disk speed is the primary bottleneck.
