Is your automation strategy still anchored to legacy scripts and outdated runtimes? In the fast-paced world of Cloud Ops, staying current isn't just a preference—it's a security and performance requirement. The latest update to Azure Automation is designed to eliminate the friction of technical debt once and for all.

The **General Availability of PowerShell 7.6 runbooks** marks a significant milestone for cloud engineers. This update enables you to seamlessly upgrade your mission-critical scripts to a modern, fully supported runtime environment. Beyond the version bump, the release introduces a massive productivity booster: native support for **Azure CLI commands** directly within your PowerShell scripts. This unified approach allows you to leverage the best of both toolsets without the typical overhead or workaround complexity.

Ready to modernize your automation environment and streamline your management stack?
